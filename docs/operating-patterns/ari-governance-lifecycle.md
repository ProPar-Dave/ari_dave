# ARI Governance Lifecycle

## Purpose

This document defines the canonical operational lifecycle used by ARI governance systems.

---

# Governance Lifecycle

```text
Intent
-> Specification
-> Governed Artifact
-> Runtime Projection
-> Mutation
-> Validation
-> Promotion
-> Drift
-> Reconciliation
-> Recovery
-> Trust Restoration
```

---

# Lifecycle Stages

## Intent

Operational goals and expectations become defined.

---

## Specification

Governed semantic expectations become formalized.

---

## Governed Artifact

Operational implementations become represented as governed entities.

---

## Runtime Projection

Governed entities become projected into operational systems.

---

## Mutation

Operational change occurs.

---

## Validation

Continuity, trust, and semantic expectations become evaluated.

---

## Promotion

Operational trust becomes elevated.

---

## Drift

Runtime divergence emerges.

---

## Reconciliation

Operational alignment becomes restored.

---

## Recovery

Trusted rollback boundaries become restored.

---

## Trust Restoration

Governed operational confidence becomes re-established.

---

# Significance

This lifecycle demonstrates how ARI models operational systems as evolving continuity-preserving governance structures.
