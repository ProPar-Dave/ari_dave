# Operational Continuity Doctrine

## Core Principle

Operational continuity is the preservation of explainable operational meaning across change.

Continuity includes:

- semantic continuity
- lineage continuity
- trust continuity
- runtime continuity
- recovery continuity

---

# Continuity Versus Regeneration

Regeneration may recreate functionality.

Continuity preserves:

- operational identity
- explainable ancestry
- governance visibility
- trusted recovery paths

These are not equivalent.

---

# Continuity and Drift

Operational continuity may degrade gradually through:

- runtime drift
- semantic drift
- lineage fragmentation
- implicit trust escalation

ARI exists partly to preserve continuity visibility.

---

# Continuity and Recovery

Recovery restores a prior trusted state.

Reconciliation restores explainable alignment.

Both contribute to operational continuity.

---

# Continuity and Governance

Governance should:

- preserve rollback capability
- preserve causality
- preserve semantic intent
- preserve explainable trust
- preserve operational identity

---

# Significance

Operational continuity is the central organizing principle of ARI.
