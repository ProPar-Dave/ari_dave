# Trust-Aware Governance Doctrine

## Core Principle

Operational trust should remain explicit, explainable, and preservable.

ARI treats trust as a governed operational property rather than an implicit assumption.

---

# Trust and Continuity

Operational continuity depends partly on trust continuity.

Systems become fragile when:

- trust assumptions become implicit
- lineage becomes incomplete
- runtime drift becomes invisible
- recovery paths become uncertain

---

# Trust and AI Systems

AI systems naturally produce:

- inferred states
- regenerated artifacts
- probabilistic outputs
- implicit transitions

Trust-aware governance exists partly to constrain those behaviors.

---

# Trust and Recovery

Recovery should preserve:

- trust visibility
- rollback explainability
- semantic continuity
- operational lineage

---

# Trust and Canonical State

Canonical states should:

- preserve reviewability
- preserve lineage
- preserve operational explainability
- preserve rollback capability

---

# Significance

Trust-aware governance is one of the foundational organizing principles of ARI.
