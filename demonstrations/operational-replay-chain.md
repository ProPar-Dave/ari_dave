# Operational Replay Chain Demonstration

## Purpose

This demonstration models how ARI reconstructs operational continuity through replayable governance events.

---

# Replay Chain

```text
Mutation
-> Drift
-> Detection
-> Rollback
-> Recovery
-> Trust Restoration
```

---

# Replayable Operational Sequence

## Mutation Event

```text
EVENT-MUTATED
```

A governed runtime mutation modifies operational behavior.

---

## Drift State

```text
STATE-DRIFTED
```

Operational divergence emerges relative to canonical expectations.

---

## Drift Detection

```text
EVENT-DRIFT-DETECTED
```

Governance-aware monitoring identifies divergence.

---

## Rollback Event

```text
EVENT-ROLLED-BACK
```

Rollback restores a previously trusted operational boundary.

---

## Recovery State

```text
STATE-RECOVERED
```

Operational continuity becomes restored.

---

## Trust Restoration

```text
STATE-CANONICAL
```

Governed trust becomes re-established.

---

# Significance

This demonstration proves that ARI can:

- reconstruct operational continuity
- preserve replayable causality
- explain recovery paths
- preserve trust transitions
- model governance-aware operational history
