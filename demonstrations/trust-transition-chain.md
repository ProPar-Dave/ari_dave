# Trust Transition Chain Demonstration

## Purpose

This demonstration models how operational trust evolves across governed transitions.

---

# Trust Evolution Chain

```text
AI-Generated
-> Candidate
-> Human Reviewed
-> Canonical
-> Drifted
-> Reconciled
-> Trusted Recovery
```

---

# Transition Semantics

## AI-Generated

Characteristics:

- low operational trust
- unverified
- lineage incomplete

---

## Candidate

Characteristics:

- reviewable
- partially explainable
- constrained by governance contracts

---

## Human Reviewed

Characteristics:

- operationally evaluated
- lineage verified
- rollback boundaries visible

---

## Canonical

Characteristics:

- trusted operational reference
- suitable for dependency usage
- governed by semantic continuity expectations

---

## Drifted

Characteristics:

- runtime divergence detected
- trust partially degraded
- reconciliation required

---

## Reconciled

Characteristics:

- operational alignment restored
- semantic continuity re-established
- trust partially restored

---

## Trusted Recovery

Characteristics:

- rollback completed successfully
- continuity restored
- governance visibility preserved

---

# Significance

This demonstration proves that ARI can model:

- trust evolution
- trust degradation
- trust restoration
- governance-aware operational transitions
- continuity-preserving recovery
