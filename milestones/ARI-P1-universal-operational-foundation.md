# ARI-P1 — Universal Operational Foundation

## Purpose

ARI-P1 represents the milestone where ARI becomes suitable for broad operational use across domains.

The milestone is not based on document volume.

The milestone is based on operational governance capability maturity.

---

# Core Objective

ARI-P1 is achieved when ARI can preserve:

- continuity
- trust
- lineage
- semantic meaning
- rollback capability
- operational explainability

across arbitrary operational systems.

---

# Readiness Capabilities

## ARI-R1 — Universal Entity Modeling

Meaningful operational entities can be modeled consistently.

---

## ARI-R2 — Stable Semantic Identity

Operational identity survives runtime evolution and mutation.

---

## ARI-R3 — Typed Relationship Graph

Operational relationships are explicit, typed, and traversable.

---

## ARI-R4 — Runtime Projection Capability

Governed entities can project into real runtime systems.

---

## ARI-R5 — Drift and Reconciliation Awareness

Drift, reconciliation, and recovery states are explainable.

---

## ARI-R6 — Trust-Aware Governance

Trust boundaries and transitions remain explicit.

---

## ARI-R7 — Causality and Replayability

Operational history can be reconstructed causally.

---

## ARI-R8 — Queryability

Operational questions can be answered deterministically.

---

## ARI-R9 — Machine-Governable Structure

Governance semantics are machine-readable and structurally consistent.

---

## ARI-R10 — Cross-Domain Portability

ARI primitives can model operational systems across domains.

---

# Required Demonstration

ARI-P1 should demonstrate at least one complete operational continuity chain.

Example:

```text
prompt
-> specification
-> governed artifact
-> deployment
-> runtime state
-> drift
-> rollback
-> recovery
-> trust restoration
```

---

# Completion Philosophy

ARI-P1 is complete when ARI behaves like a continuity-preserving operational substrate rather than a governance documentation repository.
